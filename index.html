FAQ
Q How long does it take to get a return on investment for a self-service laundromat?▾

Most CleanChain partners see a return on investment within 12–24 months, depending on location, foot traffic, and equipment mix. Our franchise team can model a projection specific to your site.

Q I don't own a commercial space. Can you help evaluate potential locations?▾

Yes. The CleanChain team has extensive experience in store expansion and location analysis. We evaluate key factors such as population density, target customer base, and local competition in your selected areas.

Choosing the right location is one of the most important factors for success, and we help identify commercial spaces with the strongest potential for long-term profitability.

Q I have a full-time job. Can I run a laundromat as a side business?▾

Absolutely. Self-service laundromats require minimal daily involvement — most owners check in remotely via the IoT app and only visit periodically for maintenance and cash collection.

Q How soon can I open my store after signing the contract?▾

Typical timelines range from 6–10 weeks from contract signing to grand opening, depending on renovation scope and equipment lead time.

Q Is there a warranty and after-sales service?▾

Yes. All equipment comes with a manufacturer warranty, and CleanChain's local technical teams provide ongoing maintenance and after-sales support.

Q How much is the franchise fee?▾

Franchise fees vary by market, store size, and equipment configuration. Our consultants will provide a detailed quotation after an initial consultation.

Q If a machine breaks down, is local maintenance fast? Will I have to wait long for spare parts?▾

CleanChain maintains a local spare parts inventory in each active market, and our technical teams aim to respond to service requests within 24–48 hours.

Q Will the laundry equipment disturb nearby neighbors?▾

Our machines are engineered for low noise and vibration during operation, and our store layout planning also factors in placement to minimize any impact on neighboring units.
